Our Home

We still love our home here in the Seattle area that we bought in 1998, and do upgrades regularly.

  • In 2021 we relandscaped the entire acre with 3 retaining walls, a new patio, and a water feature. We also added a yard irrigation system at that time.
  • In 2021 I built a garden shed for mowers, shovels, rakes, garden supplies, etc.
  • In 2023 I built a covered workshop area.
  • In 2024 we added a mini-split ac system for cooling the house it’s getting warmer here in Seattle the last decade. I also added a shade sale to our lower patio.
  • This past summer, I built a new shed for my new cub cadet lawn tractor (we have an acre of property) and to store all of Brenda’s wedding decorations.
  • This winter I installed a battery backup system for our sump pump. We had that atmospheric river hit us in November and December, and the 10 year old sump pump died. When I realized that if we lost power during one of those, my basement would have had 3 feet of water in it overnight, I decided that I better do something about that!
  • We also got a new dinette set this Summer (Amish made, it is amazing), and we finally replaced our aging refrigerator.
